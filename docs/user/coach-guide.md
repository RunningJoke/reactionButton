# Training with ReactPods: a guide for coaches

How to plan sessions with ReactPods: where to put the pods, which game fits which goal, and
how to run a group. For switching on, the light codes and the app, see the
[user manual](manual.md). Deutsch: [Trainerleitfaden](trainerleitfaden.md).

---

## 1. Before the session

- **Charge the pods.** A charge lasts about an hour of play. Charge them switched on, and
  never unattended.
- **Check the set.** Switch all pods on once at home: every target must turn yellow and join
  when you start a game. A pod that stays yellow belongs to another set (manual §5).
- **Choose the central.** It is the pod the athlete presses most in many games (Weiche, Stern,
  Dreizack). Put it where you want the athlete to come back to.
- **Plan the games.** Each game needs a fixed number of pods (manual §6). With five pods you
  can play every shipped game.
- **Optional: the app.** Use it to change the number of rounds, sort the menu so that today's
  games come first, and add the athletes you will record.

## 2. Placing the pods

| Setup | Distance | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| On a table or wall, within arm's reach | 30–60 cm | Hand speed, eye–hand coordination, rehab |
| On the floor in a half circle | 1–3 m | Footwork, quick steps, goalkeepers |
| Spread over a court | 5–15 m | Sprints, change of direction, team games |
| On cones or walls at different heights | – | Looking around, hands and feet mixed |

Tips:

- Put the pods where the athlete can **see all of them** without turning. Peripheral vision
  is part of the training; a pod behind the athlete turns it into a search game.
- Keep the same layout for a series of sessions. Only then do the times compare.
- Tell the athlete how to press (flat hand, fingers) and keep it the same: it changes the
  times a lot.
- Radio range is enough for one hall. Walls or many bodies between the pods can make a pod
  drop out for a moment: the central turns yellow and the game waits until it is back.

## 3. Which game for which goal

| Goal | Games | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Pure reaction** | Solo, Release | Solo: press when the light turns green; false starts show red. Release: let go on green, like a sprint start. |
| **Choice reaction** | Run5 | Green and red targets at once: press only the green ones. A red press ends the round. |
| **Footwork, coming back to the middle** | Weiche, Dreizack, Stern | Central in the middle, targets around it: target, centre, target, centre … |
| **Moving between points** | Dreieck, Quadrat | One pod lights at random, never the same twice in a row. 12 rounds per cycle. |
| **Speed over a course** | Run | All pods light at once; press them all as fast as possible. The app shows the lap time. |
| **Partner / rhythm** | Duo | Central and target take turns; two athletes can share it. Missing for 5 s counts as a miss. |
| **Timing, holding** | Charge | Hold to "charge" the ring and let go at the right moment. |
| **Team game** | Domination | Two teams flip pods between red and blue. Most pods after 20 s wins. |

Start with the simpler games (Solo, Duo, Dreieck) for beginners and children. The
"come back to the middle" games are tiring: plan short cycles and enough rest.

## 4. A sample session (about 45 minutes)

| Time | Part | Game and setup |
|---|---|---|
| 5 min | Get to know the pods | Solo on a table: everyone tries a few presses, learns green = press, red = don't. |
| 10 min | Warm-up | Dreieck on the floor, 2 m apart, easy pace. One cycle each, swap. |
| 15 min | Main part: footwork | Stern or Dreizack with the central in the middle, 2–3 m. 2–3 cycles per athlete, at least the same time as rest. |
| 10 min | Choice reaction | Run5 over a short course: press only the green ones. |
| 5 min | Game to finish | Domination, two teams. |

Between cycles the pods show **all blue** for a few seconds, then the next cycle starts by
itself. That is the moment to swap athletes.

## 5. Running a group

- **One set per station.** Pods only join their own set, so several sets can train in one
  hall. Give each set a name in the app ("Station 1", "Hall A"). All sets still share one
  radio channel; with many sets close together a pod may drop out for a moment more often
  (a channel per set is planned).
- **Stations with rotation.** For example three stations with one set each: reaction (Solo),
  footwork (Weiche), course (Run). Swap stations after each cycle.
- **Waiting athletes count or judge.** With Run5 they can watch for wrong presses; with Duo two
  athletes play against each other.
- **Change the game** by switching the central off and on (holding the top during the blue
  light) and choosing again. The targets can stay on.

## 6. Measuring progress

- The pods measure every press. After each cycle they work out best, average and worst time.
- With firmware 2.12 or newer and the app connected, the results arrive **live**: pick the
  athlete in the app before the cycle; the app saves the result to that athlete. After the
  cycle you choose **Continue**, **Repeat** (the result is not counted) or **Stop**
  (manual §8.6).
- In the app the **Pause** button pauses the game, for example to explain something. Only
  **Resume** in the app continues it; until then the pods stay yellow.
- Compare only results from the **same game, layout and distance**. The average says more
  than the best time; a better worst time shows that the athlete stays focused.
- Children and beginners improve quickly in the first sessions just from knowing the game.
  Real progress shows after a few weeks.

## 7. Safety

- Check the floor around the pods: no slipping on pods placed on the floor, enough room for
  stopping after a sprint.
- Pods on walls or tables: fix them so they cannot fall.
- Plan rest. Reaction training is intense; quality drops quickly when athletes are tired.
- Never leave pods charging unattended.
- Results of athletes are personal data. Record them only with consent (for children, the
  parents' consent).

## 8. Quick answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How many athletes per set? | One at a time. Several athletes take turns. |
| Can I play without a phone? | Yes, every shipped game runs without the app. You only see no numbers. |
| Can I change the number of rounds without the app? | No. Use the app (manual §8.1). |
| A pod went yellow during the game | A target is lost (battery, range, switched off). The game continues when it is back. |
| The athlete pressed too early | Solo shows red (false start) and records no time for that try. |
