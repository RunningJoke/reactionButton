Source for the wiki manual page "Light language" (firmware 2.15, #82). Paste the table and the colour line into the wiki as they are.

| What the coach sees | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Ring fills blue, then white (about 2.6 s) after switching on | Pod is starting. Blue fill = battery level; amber fill = below 20 % | Hold the button until the white fill ends: this pod becomes the central. Released earlier: it is a peripheral |
| Amber light bounces over 10 of the 18 lights | Pod is on but not in a set yet | Start the set's central, the pod joins by itself. To move the pod to another set: hold its button 3 s (white flash) |
| A few lights in the pod's colour flash twice, then stay dim; 10 lights breathe green | Pod is in the set and the central is in menu, discovery or confirm. The number of lit lights is the pod number | Use colour and count to find the pod |
| Central shows its program pattern, slowly breathing | A phone is connected to the central | Nothing |
| Pod lights go dark | Game has started | Nothing. The game controls the lights |
| One amber light blinks once a second | Pod battery is below 15 % | Charge the pod soon |
| Central ring breathes green | All pods are here, game is ready | Press the central's button to start |
| Central ring flashes red for 1 s after the start press | A pod's battery is below 5 %, the game does not start | Charge that pod, then press again |
| Central ring fills white clockwise while you hold its button | Program is being selected | Keep holding until the ring is full (about 1.8 s). Let go earlier to cancel |
| White drains back anticlockwise | Selection cancelled | Nothing. Choose a program again |
| Ring flashes white twice | Program selected, looking for the pods | Wait for the pod lights |
| First 8 lights show one dim light per pod, in its colour | Pod not found yet | Switch the pod on. If it stays dim, press its button once (it may be asleep) |
| The same lights are bright | Pod found | Nothing |
| Central ring breathes amber | Game is paused: a pod is out of range, or the app paused it | Check the pod (power, distance). The game resumes by itself when the pod is back. If the app paused it, resume in the app |
| Central ring blinks red, 1 s on and 1 s off | No program, or a program could not be loaded | Upload the programs, fix the broken one, reboot the central |
| Central blinks white for 2 s | Identify: this pod is the central | Nothing |
| Ring white drains anticlockwise and dims to dark | Auto power-off after 10 min idle | Press the button to switch on again |

Pod colours: pod 1 blue, pod 2 cyan, pod 3 violet, pod 4 magenta, pod 5 yellow, pod 6 orange, pod 7 cream. The central has no number and is white.
